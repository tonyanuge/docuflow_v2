Develop a comprehensive authentication system for Unseen, structured around three key phases: Multi-Factor Authentication, LinkedIn Account Verification, and AI-Based Identity & Profile Photo Verification.

Step 1: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Objective: Implement secure login and authentication via a secondary verification method.
Actions:

Set up the standard username/password login process.
Integrate an SMS or email verification API, such as Twilio or Firebase Authentication.
Alternatively, provide support for authenticator apps like Google Authenticator or Authy.
Clearly guide users during signup or login to complete MFA setup.
Technology Tools: Twilio, Firebase Auth, Google Authenticator SDK/API.
Outcome: Ensures users confirm their identities through a secondary verification method upon each login, enhancing security.
Step 2: LinkedIn (or Social) Account Verification (Optional but Encouraged)
Objective: Establish credibility and authenticity by allowing users to verify identities through reputable third-party platforms like LinkedIn.
Actions:

Obtain LinkedIn OAuth credentials by registering your app on the LinkedIn Developers Portal.
Integrate LinkedIn’s OAuth 2.0 authentication flow into your login process.
Provide clear prompts during registration to connect or link their LinkedIn account.
Securely store verified LinkedIn data; specify publicly visible data (job title, industry) while keeping sensitive information private.
Technology Tools: LinkedIn API, OAuth 2.0 authentication libraries (OAuth.io, Passport.js for Node, Firebase Authentication).
Outcome: Users receive visible “LinkedIn verified” badges on their profiles, increasing trustworthiness.
Step 3: AI-Based Identity & Profile Photo Verification
Objective: Prevent fake profiles using AI-driven facial recognition and identity verification.
Actions:

Integrate facial recognition or biometric APIs, such as Amazon Rekognition, Azure Face API, or Face++ API.
Prompt users during account setup or profile photo update to submit a live selfie for verification.
Use AI facial analysis to compare the live selfie against uploaded profile photos.
Implement a verification status indicator (e.g., “Verified Profile”) once identity is confirmed.
Provide manual review fallback for cases flagged in verification.
Outcome: Reduces the prevalence of fake profiles through reliable AI-driven verification.
